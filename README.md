# P2 Opportunity Assessment Tool (SPPIGOT)

A web-based tool for municipal, tribal, and territorial governments to assess pollution prevention (P2) opportunities in their stormwater operations.

## Overview

The **Stormwater Pollution Prevention in Government Operations Tool (SPPIGOT)** helps communities:

1. **Screen** 6 types of government operations for P2 relevance
2. **Assess** current pollution prevention practices via targeted questions
3. **Identify** recommended actions filtered by pollutant type and co-benefits

### Government Operations Covered

- Parks & Open Space Management
- Winter Road Maintenance
- Construction & Maintenance
- Vehicle & Fleet Management
- Outdoor Facility Management
- Policies & Regulations

## Tech Stack

- React 19 + TypeScript
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS v4
